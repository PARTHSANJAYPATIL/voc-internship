Chocolate Cake

⏱ Preparation Time: 45 Minutes

🍽 Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Flour
  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • ½ Cup Cocoa Powder
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • ½ Cup Butter
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix all dry ingredients together.
  3. Add eggs, milk and butter.
  4. Mix until smooth.
  5. Pour into a baking tray.
  6. Bake for 35 minutes.
  7. Cool and serve.
